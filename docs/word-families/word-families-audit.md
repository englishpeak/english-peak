# Word Families: editorial audit

Reviewed all 300 records for category, morphology, teaching value, spelling, and target consistency. 87 records changed. This is a pedagogical editorial review, not a corpus-frequency or CEFR certification. Dictionary spot-checks support the disputed cases; not every word was individually dictionary-verified.

Blocked means outside this exercise’s selected targets, not that no English derivative exists. Inflected participles are retained where they have useful adjectival use (for example, experienced, unemployed, written); weak filler participles are removed. The two adjective columns represent two target groups, not every possible adjective.
